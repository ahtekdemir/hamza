Fonts used in the reel, all under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (free for commercial use),
taken from the Fontsource packages of the Google Fonts originals:

- Sancreek (Vernon Adams) – headlines
- Rye (Nicole Fally) – artwork on the sample transfers
- Anton (Vernon Adams) – artwork on the sample transfers
- Montserrat (Julieta Ulanovsky et al.) – labels
- Playfair Display Italic (Claus Eggers Sørensen) – accent lines
- anton-typeface.json – Anton outlines for 3D lettering, converted with scripts/make-typeface.mjs
- Pacifico (Vernon Adams et al.) – script lettering on the Thanksgiving transfers and end card
